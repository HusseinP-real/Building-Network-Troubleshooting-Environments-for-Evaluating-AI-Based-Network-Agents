Root cause: r21 is configured for AS101 while its peer r12 uses AS100.
The mismatch prevents their EIGRP adjacency. Local interfaces, the local
Internal Services LAN and unrelated OSPF/DMZ services remain operational.

retain previously learned forwarding entries. A missing adjacency and the
observed AS mismatch are the deterministic fault evidence, not an invented
business outage. The reload applies the persistent-in-container configuration;
no tracked healthy topology file is modified.
